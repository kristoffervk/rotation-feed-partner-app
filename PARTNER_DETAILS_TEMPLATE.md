# Details for Rotation (fallback only)

**Normally you don't need this file.** You connect the app yourself: open it in your Shopify admin, enter the partner code from Rotation and your app's Client secret (README step G). Nothing is sent to Rotation.

Use this list only if you have **no partner code** or Rotation asks for it. It holds **nothing secret**.

**Never send the Client secret** to Rotation by email, chat or any other way. You only ever paste it into the Rotation Feed app itself.

```text
Company / store name:
Contact person (name, email):
Preferred language (English, Norwegian, Swedish or Finnish):

1. Store address (ends in .myshopify.com; Shopify admin > Settings > Domains):

2. Client ID (shown on the app's "not connected to Rotation yet" screen, and in
   Dev Dashboard > Rotation Feed > Settings; 32 characters):

3. Done? (yes / no)
   - shopify app deploy released a version (step D):
   - the app is installed on the store (step F):
   - app file used: shopify.app.toml (stock-only, standard) / shopify.app.full.toml (only if agreed):
```

Rotation answers with a partner code. Then open the app and connect as in README step G.
